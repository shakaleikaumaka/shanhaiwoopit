# 🎁 Dear ShanHaiWoo 山海坞 — and every friend of the mountain-sea

For a few weeks in the fall of 2024, a corner of Shanghai became a village.
Builders, researchers, artists and zu-friends gathered between the mountains
and the sea — and for a little while, the future had an address.

This is **The ShanHaiWoo P.I.T.** — a Public Information Transmission
instance, P.I.T. №17, forked with aloha from The VitPit. It gathers the
village's own recordings — the opening ceremony keynote, the cowork opening,
an interview, and the ZuTown Hall — alongside its photographs and its paper
trail, so the whole gathering can travel in a pocket.

It is a gift, and like every pit it is honest about what it is:

- **It is:** the village's transponder — four sessions carried as
  cross-exhibits via The VitPit, the gallery, the documents, the lore.
  ≈$0 infrastructure. Everything CC0.
- **It isn't:** finished. Transcripts, full credits and exact dates are
  queued to the pipeline — the pit marks them *pending* rather than
  inventing them. Declared means declared, not done.

And it lives by the protocol's fifth stage, its soul: **consent-first
governance**. If you appear in this footage and would rather not, one email —
consent@publicinform.com — and you're gone. No questions, honored with love.

The mountain doesn't rush, and neither does the sea. But both remember.
